Collection: Doctor Who (1963)
=============================

:: Title
Doctor Who

:: Date
1963-1989

:: Description
Doctor Who is a British science fiction television series first broadcast by
the BBC in 1963. The series, created by Sydney Newman, C. E. Webber and
Donald Wilson, follows the adventures of the Doctor, an extraterrestrial
being from a humanoid species known as Time Lords. The Doctor travels through
space and time using a time traveling spaceship called the TARDIS, which has
an exterior that resembles a British police box. The Doctor encounters
various civilizations, which they seek to protect by outwitting foes and
solving crises. The Doctor usually travels with a companion.

:: Title
The Masque of Mandragora

:: Date
1976-09-04

:: Description
The serial is set in the fictional European duchy of San Martino in the late
15th century. In the serial, the astrologer Hieronymous seeks to summon the
power of an intelligence called the Mandragora Helix to rule the Earth.

drwho1963e14x02
===============

:: Title
The Hand of Fear

:: Date
1976-10-02

:: Description
Sarah Jane Smith is caught in a quarry explosion and found clutching a
fossilised hand. The hand belongs to Eldrad, a Kastrian criminal executed
millions of years ago, who takes control of Sarah and regenerates by draining
the radiation of a nuclear power station. Sarah's final serial as a regular
companion.

===============

:: Title
The Deadly Assassin

:: Date
1976-10-30

:: Description
The Doctor returns alone to Gallifrey and is framed for the assassination of
the Time Lord President. To clear his name he enters the Matrix, the Time
Lords' virtual repository of knowledge, where he battles an unseen enemy. The
plot is the work of the Master, now at the end of his regenerations.

drwho1963e14x04
===============

:: Title
The Face of Evil

:: Date
1977-01-01

:: Description
The Doctor lands on a planet where the Sevateem tribe wage holy war against the
Tesh at the command of their god Xoanon. Xoanon turns out to be a ship's
computer the Doctor once repaired, unwittingly imprinting it with his own
personality and driving it mad. The Doctor meets Leela, a warrior of the
Sevateem cast out for heresy.

1977-02-26

:: Description
In Victorian London, the Doctor and Leela investigate the disappearance of
