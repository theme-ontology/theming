Collection: Doctor Who (1963)

:: Title
Doctor Who

:: Date
1963-1989

:: Description
Doctor Who is a British science fiction television series first broadcast by
the BBC in 1963. The series, created by Sydney Newman, C. E. Webber and
Donald Wilson, follows the adventures of the Doctor, an extraterrestrial
being from a humanoid species known as Time Lords. The Doctor travels through
space and time using a time traveling spaceship called the TARDIS, which has
an exterior that resembles a British police box. The Doctor encounters
various civilizations, which they seek to protect by outwitting foes and
solving crises. The Doctor usually travels with a companion.

:: Title
An Unearthly Child

:: Date
1976-09-04

:: Description
Schoolteachers Ian Chesterton and Barbara Wright are concerned about one of their pupils, Susan Foreman. When they visit her address to investigate, they encounter a police box and hear Susan's voice inside. An old man arrives, but refuses to let the teachers inside the police box; when they enter, they discover it is much bigger on the inside. Susan explains that the object is a time and space machine called the TARDIS and the old man is her grandfather, the Doctor. Refusing to let Ian and Barbara leave, the Doctor sets the TARDIS in flight and ends up in the Stone Age. After exiting the TARDIS, tribe man Kal witnesses the Doctor light a match and takes back to the tribe; Ian, Barbara, and Susan intervene, but the group is imprisoned in a large cave. Kal says they will be sacrificed if they do not make fire. While Ian tries to start a fire, Kal enters the cave and attacks the leader Za, but is killed. Ian gives a burning torch to Za, who shows it to the tribe and is declared leader. The group flee to the TARDIS and escape through time and space to a silent and unknown forest.

15th century. In the serial, the astrologer Hieronymous seeks to summon the

:: Title
The Hand of Fear

:: Date
1976-10-02

:: Description
Sarah Jane Smith is caught in a quarry explosion and found clutching a
fossilized hand. The hand belongs to Eldrad, a Kastrian criminal executed
millions of years ago, who takes control of Sarah and regenerates by draining
the radiation of a nuclear power station. Sarah's final serial as a regular
companion.

:: Ratings
4 <paul>
3 <mikael>

:: Choice Themes
the lust for power [Eldrad ultimately revealed that his true motive for returning to Kastria was to seize control of the planet and use it as a springboard from which to conquer the entire universe.]
attack from outer space by a powerful conquering enemy [The Doctor had to remove Eldrad from Earth because of the threat the alien posed to humanity. Indeed, the story concluded with Eldrad intending to go back and make himself ruler of Earth. Humanity was saved by the Doctor's iconic scarf that, carefully rigged, tripped Eldrad to fall into a bottomless chasm.]

:: Major Themes
mind control technology [Eldrad used a will subverting ring to mind control Sarah and others into doing his bidding.]
nuclear power safety [The near melting down of the Nunton Complex nuclear reactor confronts the viewer with the hot button issue at the time of whether nuclear power is safe.]
being that feeds on something intangible [Eldrad, who started out as a fossilized limestone hand, gradually regenerated his entire body by absorbing radiation.]
silicon-based being [Eldrad was a crystalline life form based on silicon.]
the desire for vengeance [Eldrad wanted to avenge his obliteration 150 million years prior.]
extinct interstellar civilization [It was revealed that the Kastrians had chosen extinction rather than endure a permanent existence underground after Eldrad had rendered the surface of their home world uninhabitable.]
speculative healing device [Eldrad was almost indestructible because he had a ring imprinted with his genetic code that used radiation to rebuild his body as needed.]

nuclear fission power [Part of the story took place in a nuclear power plant near the reactor core, and pivoted on its radiation.]

:: Minor Themes

speculative hypnosis [The Doctor hypnotized Sarah against her will to probe her unconscious mind for information on Eldrad.]

the dangers of radioactivity [The nuclear facility people worried that Sarah had been exposed to enough radiation to "kill a herd of elephants".]

The Doctor returns alone to Gallifrey and is framed for the assassination of
the Time Lord President. To clear his name he enters the Matrix, the Time

plot is the work of the Master, now at the end of his regenerations.
