:: Title
Devs

:: Date
2020

:: Description
Devs is an American science fiction thriller miniseries created, written
and directed by Alex Garland. Its eight episodes premiered on FX on Hulu
between March 5 and April 16, 2020. Lily Chan, a software engineer at the
quantum computing company Amaya, becomes caught up in the death of her
boyfriend, who died on his first day working for Amaya's secretive Devs
division.

:: Title
Episode 1

:: Date
2020-03-05

:: Description
Lily Chan's boyfriend Sergei is given a coveted position on the secretive
quantum computing team at Amaya, called Devs. After being shown what the
machine can do, he secretly records its code using his wristwatch. Forest,
Amaya's founder, confronts him over the theft and has Kenton, his head of
security, suffocate him. Lily reports Sergei missing and is shown footage
of him apparently walking out of the building and setting himself on fire.
On his phone she finds a program disguised as a Sudoku game, and asks her
ex-boyfriend Jamie to break into it.

:: Major Themes
boyfriend and girlfriend [The story follows Lily and Sergei, a young coder couple who live together in a San Francisco apartment. Lily became convinced that something bad had happened to Sergei when he didn't return home from work after accepting a position with a secretive quantum computing team at the Amaya corporation. Lily's worst fear was seemingly confirmed when she was shown security camera footage of what appeared to be Sergei taking his own life by the simple expedient of self-immolation.]

:: Title
Episode 8

:: Date
2020-04-16

:: Description
Stewart recites W. B. Yeats's "The Second Coming" at the entrance to Devs.
Forest shows Lily the machine's projection of what is about to happen: she
forces him into the lab's capsule at gunpoint and shoots him, and the
capsule falls, killing them both. Lily instead throws the gun away,
breaking the projection. Stewart crashes the capsule regardless, and Forest
and Lily die. Lily wakes inside a simulation in which Devs was never built,
and Forest is with his wife and daughter, alive. Katie shows Devs to
Senator Laine and asks her to keep it running.

:: Ratings
3 <mikael>

:: Choice Themes
digital afterlife [Somewhat incongrously, the story concluded with the Devs/Deus quantum computer which could see into the past and the future with ease, somehow being used to simulate a digital afterlife for Forest and Lily in a version of the universe in which their respective loved ones hadn't brutally perished. The conciousnesses of Forest and Lily were aparently transported into a simulatino of that reality from their original one at the moment of their deaths. How and why the computer was able to simulate the unique new reality that emerged from such an intrusion, was quietly left as an exercise for the viewer.]
