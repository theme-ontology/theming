cryptography
============

:: Description
A technique for secure communication in the presence of a third party is
featured. These are usually strategies for writing messages such that they can 
only be read by those who know the secret to decoding them.

:: Parents
science

:: Examples
Sherlock Holmes and Watson cracked a book cipher in sherlock1x02 "The Blind
Banker".

:: References
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptography

parallel universe
=================

:: Description
There is exactly two distinct universes that exist in parallel.
Perhaps each is the "mirror" version of the other.

:: Parents
multiverse

:: Notes
The word "dimension" is used in two different sense in fiction. Sometimes what
is meant is no different from a parallel universe. Sometimes what is meant is
a fourth spatial dimension of space as an addition to the three we know. In
the latter case we use "four-dimensional space" as a theme.

:: References
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_universes_in_fiction
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiverse

quantum mechanics
=================

:: Description
Quantum mechanics, also known as quantum physics, is the fundamental physical theory that describes the behavior of matter and of light; the behaviors it models typically occur at and below the scale of atoms, and have often been described as counterintuitive.
Some or all of this is discussed in the story.
